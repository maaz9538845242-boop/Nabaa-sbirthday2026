Dear Naba, ❤️

I don’t know if I’ve ever told you how beautiful I have always found you. And perhaps that’s one of the many things I never said enough. I still remember how it all began. You were just someone on my Instagram, someone I found myself looking at a little longer than I probably should have. I liked you from the very beginning, even before we started talking properly. But there was a part of me you probably didn’t completely understand. I had just come out of a relationship, and I was not looking for love anymore. I had convinced myself that being alone was easier than trusting someone again. I wasn’t ready to give someone the power to hurt me. And honestly, I had made peace with the idea of never committing to anyone again. Then you came along. We started talking, slowly got closer, and somewhere between those conversations, you became someone I genuinely looked forward to. I liked you more than I wanted to admit. But I was scared. I remember how you convinced me that you would be there, that you wouldn’t hurt me, that I didn’t have to be afraid of loving again. And somehow, I believed you. For someone who had built so many walls around his heart, letting you in wasn’t a small thing. It was probably one of the bravest things I had done for myself in a long time. And that’s why, even after everything that happened, some questions still visit me when the world gets quiet. I sometimes wonder whether those promises were real. Whether you meant every word when you told me I was safe with you. Whether the person who convinced me to believe in love again ever understood how much it took for me to believe. I don’t ask these questions because I want answers today. And I certainly don’t want to make your birthday about the things that hurt. I just wish you knew that what I felt was real. That the trust I gave you was real. And that the person who once thought he would never love again genuinely tried. And now, let me tell you something I should have told you openly a long time ago. You are beautiful, Naba. Not just in the usual way people compliment someone. There’s something about your face that I have always admired. Your smile, your eyes, the little details that make you you. And that nose ring… I don’t think you realise how much I loved that little detail about you. It suited you so beautifully. I probably noticed it a hundred times and said it far too few. I wish I had complimented you more. I wish I hadn’t kept so many lovely things to myself. Today, I don’t want to remind you of sadness, make you feel guilty, or ask you to explain anything. I just want you to feel celebrated. I hope Berlin gives you beautiful mornings, unexpected adventures, warm hugs, and the kind of happiness that stays long after the day ends. I hope you never stop smiling, never doubt your beauty, and never stop becoming the person you want to be. And if there’s one thing I hope you remember, it’s this: Some people don’t realise how difficult it was for someone to open their heart again. But you were once the person who made me want to try. Happy Birthday, Naba. ❤️ May life be gentle with you. May your dreams find you. And may you always have a reason to look at yourself in the mirror and smile. With a heart that once found the courage to try again, From India, with warmth. 🌷
